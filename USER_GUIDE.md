## Creating Your First Estimate

### Step 1: Capture Your Driveway

**Option A: Take a Photo (Mobile)**
1. Click "New Estimate" or "Create Project"
2. Select "Take Photo"
3. Grant camera permissions if prompted
4. Stand back and capture your entire driveway
5. Ensure good lighting for best AI detection

**Option B: Upload Photo (Desktop/Mobile)**
1. Click "New Estimate" or "Create Project"
2. Select "Upload Photo"
3. Choose an existing photo from your device
4. Ensure the photo shows the complete driveway

### Step 2: AI-Powered Measurement

The AI automatically detects driveway boundaries:
- **Green corners** show detected edges
- Review the detected area
- The square footage is calculated automatically

### Step 3: Fine-Tune Measurements

If needed, adjust the corner points:
1. **Drag corners** to match your driveway edges exactly
2. Watch the square footage update in real-time
3. Ensure the area covers only the driveway surface
4. Exclude surrounding grass, sidewalks, or landscaping

### Step 4: Enter Depth Measurement

Enter the depth of your driveway in inches:
- **Standard residential**: 3-4 inches
- **Heavy-duty**: 4-6 inches
- Consult with your contractor for specific recommendations

### Step 5: Select Material

Choose from available materials:
- **Hot Mix Asphalt** - Premium, durable option
- **Asphalt Millings** - Budget-friendly recycled material
- **Tar & Chip** - Rustic appearance with good traction
- **Gravel** - Most economical option

Each material shows:
- Price per ton
- Price per square foot
- Sample preview images

### Step 6: Review Your Estimate

Your estimate includes:
- **Total square footage**
- **Material quantity needed** (in tons)
- **Cost per ton**
- **Total estimated cost**
- **Material preview** (AI-generated visualization)

### Step 7: Save Your Project

1. Enter a project name (e.g., "Front Driveway")
2. Add notes for reference
3. Click "Save Project"
4. Your project is now available in your dashboard

## Understanding Materials and Pricing

### Material Types

#### Hot Mix Asphalt
- **Best for**: High-traffic areas, main driveways
- **Durability**: 15-20 years with proper maintenance
- **Appearance**: Clean, uniform black surface
- **Cost**: Premium pricing
- **Maintenance**: Seal coating every 3-5 years

#### Asphalt Millings
- **Best for**: Budget projects, rural driveways
- **Durability**: 10-15 years
- **Appearance**: Varied, textured surface
- **Cost**: Most affordable asphalt option
- **Maintenance**: Periodic grading and compaction

#### Tar & Chip
- **Best for**: Rustic aesthetic, secondary driveways
- **Durability**: 12-18 years
- **Appearance**: Stone-textured surface
- **Cost**: Mid-range pricing
- **Maintenance**: Resealing every 5-7 years

#### Gravel
- **Best for**: Low-budget, temporary, or rural driveways
- **Durability**: 5-10 years
- **Appearance**: Natural stone look
- **Cost**: Most economical option
- **Maintenance**: Regular grading and replenishment

### Pricing Factors

Our pricing considers:
- **Your location** (regional material costs)
- **Current market rates** (updated regularly)
- **Material type** (different base costs)
- **Quantity needed** (based on your measurements)

**Note**: Final costs may vary based on:
- Site preparation requirements
- Drainage considerations
- Local contractor rates
- Permit requirements

## Troubleshooting

### Camera Not Working

**Problem**: Camera won't open or shows error

**Solutions**:
1. Check camera permissions in device settings
2. Close other apps using the camera
3. Restart the app
4. Try uploading a photo instead

### AI Detection Inaccurate

**Problem**: Detected corners don't match driveway

**Solutions**:
1. Manually adjust corner points
2. Ensure photo shows complete driveway
3. Improve lighting conditions
4. Take photo from a different angle

### Pricing Seems Wrong

**Problem**: Prices don't match local rates

**Solutions**:
1. Verify your ZIP code is correct
2. Check location services are enabled
3. Remember estimates are material costs only
4. Labor and installation costs vary by contractor

### App Won't Load

**Problem**: App stuck or shows errors

**Solutions**:
1. Check internet connection
2. Refresh the page or restart app
3. Clear browser cache (web only)
4. Update to latest app version (mobile)

### Permission Errors

**Problem**: App requests permissions repeatedly

**Solutions**:
1. Grant requested permissions
2. Check device settings for app permissions
3. Reinstall the app (mobile)
4. Use browser instead of app (temporary)

## FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: Is Driveway Estimator Pro free to use?**
A: Yes, creating estimates and sharing projects is completely free.

**Q: How accurate are the measurements?**
A: Our AI is highly accurate for standard rectangular and L-shaped driveways. Complex shapes may require manual adjustment.

**Q: Can I use estimates for contractor quotes?**
A: Yes, our PDF exports provide professional estimates you can share with contractors.

**Q: Do estimates include labor costs?**
A: No, estimates cover material costs only. Labor varies by contractor and location.

### Technical Questions

**Q: What happens to my photos?**
A: Photos are stored securely and used only for your estimates. We respect your privacy.

**Q: Is my data shared with third parties?**
A: Your project data is never shared. Only shared project links are accessible to contractors you specify.

**Q: Can I export my data?**
A: Yes, you can download PDF summaries of any project.

**Q: How often are prices updated?**
A: Regional pricing is updated regularly to reflect current market rates.

### Mobile-Specific Questions

**Q: Does the app work offline?**
A: No, an internet connection is required for AI features and real-time pricing.

**Q: How do I enable camera permissions?**
A: The app will prompt you when needed. You can also enable them in your device settings.

**Q: Is the app available for both iOS and Android?**
A: Yes, native apps are available for both platforms.
